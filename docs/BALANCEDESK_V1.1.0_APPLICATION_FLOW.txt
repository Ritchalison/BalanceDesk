BALANCEDESK v1.1.0 - APPLICATION FLOW AND CALCULATION GUIDE
============================================================

Version: 1.1.0
Runtime: Local-only Windows x64 application
Database: Local SQLite database
Currency: Ghana cedis (GHS), stored internally as whole pesewas

PURPOSE
-------
BalanceDesk records one business's daily MoMo activity, credit sales,
customer credit payments, expenses, physical closing counts, reconciliation,
and the final locking of a Business Day.

BalanceDesk does not record individual Cash Sales. Instead, Cash Sales are
calculated at the end of the day from the physical cash counted in the drawer.


1. FIRST-TIME APPLICATION SETUP
-------------------------------
1. Extract the complete client ZIP to a normal local Windows folder.
2. Run "Setup BalanceDesk.bat" once.
3. Setup creates:
   - private local security settings;
   - a new empty local database;
   - one BalanceDesk desktop shortcut with the BalanceDesk logo.
4. Setup displays a private first-run Administrator setup code. Copy it from
   the setup window when you are ready to complete the Administrator form.
5. On the setup screen, enter the business name, Administrator username,
   Administrator password, and the displayed setup code.
6. After the first Administrator is created, normal users see the login screen.

New and reset passwords must:
- contain at least 8 characters, including an uppercase letter, lowercase letter,
  number, and symbol;
- avoid obvious letter, number, and keyboard sequences or repeated patterns;
- avoid common password words; and
- not be based on the user's name, username, business name, or BalanceDesk.

The form shows these checks as you type. They are also checked again securely
when the form is submitted.

You do not run setup every day. Use the BalanceDesk desktop shortcut to open
the application whenever you need it.


2. USER ROLES
-------------
STANDARD USER
- Records MoMo Sales.
- Records Credit Sales.
- Records Cash or MoMo Credit Payments.
- Records Cash or MoMo Expenses.
- Submits the physical closing cash count and actual closing MoMo balance.
- Can view permitted customer information.
- Can cancel an eligible record that the user created while the day is Open.

ADMINISTRATOR
- Has the permitted operational functions of a Standard user.
- Opens Business Days.
- Reviews reconciliation and performs Final Close.
- Returns a day to Counts or Operations when corrections are required.
- Reopens eligible Closed Days.
- Corrects opening values with a recorded reason.
- Reviews customer classifications and possible duplicates.
- Performs later payment allocations and allocation reversals.
- Views Closed Days and Activity History.
- Creates, deactivates, reactivates, and resets passwords for Standard users.

There is no role switch inside one session. Sign out and sign in using the
appropriate account.


3. BUSINESS DAY STATES
----------------------
A Business Day normally moves through these stages:

NO ACTIVE DAY
    |
    v
OPEN
    |
    v
COUNTS SUBMITTED
    |
    v
RECONCILIATION CONFIRMED
    |
    v
CLOSED

If a correction is required before Final Close, an Administrator can return the
day to Counts or Operations. A Closed Day can be moved into a controlled
REOPENED state by an Administrator when no other Business Day is active.


4. OPENING A BUSINESS DAY
-------------------------
Only an Administrator opens a Business Day.

The Administrator enters:
- Business Date;
- Drawer Cash;
- Drawer Coins; and
- Opening MoMo Balance.

Opening Drawer Float is calculated as:

    Opening Drawer Float = Drawer Cash + Drawer Coins

Example:
    Drawer Cash                  GHS 1,000.00
    Drawer Coins                 GHS    50.00
    Opening Drawer Float         GHS 1,050.00

Important rules:
- Only one active Business Day is allowed.
- Opening fields start blank for every new day.
- Yesterday's opening figures are not automatically carried forward.
- Opening values become locked after opening in the normal workflow.
- A Standard user cannot change opening values.
- An Administrator can later use the controlled Correct Opening Values action,
  but must provide a reason; the before-and-after values remain in the audit.


5. RECORDING DAILY OPERATIONS
-----------------------------
Operations can normally be recorded while the Business Day is Open.

5.1 MOMO SALE
- Record every MoMo Sale individually.
- Enter provider, amount, date/time, and reference when available.
- A MoMo Sale:
  - increases the MoMo Sales total;
  - increases Expected Closing MoMo; and
  - contributes to Accounted Sales.

5.2 CREDIT SALE
- Every Credit Sale belongs to a customer.
- Select an existing customer or create a Provisional customer.
- Enter invoice/reference, amount, due date, and description.
- A Credit Sale:
  - increases the customer's outstanding balance;
  - contributes to Accounted Sales on the sale's Business Day; and
  - does not increase today's Cash or MoMo balance until payment is received.

5.3 CREDIT PAYMENT
- Use Credit Payment when a customer pays an earlier credit balance.
- Select Cash or MoMo as the payment channel.
- Allocate the payment to an invoice when the intended invoice is known.
- A Credit Payment:
  - reduces the customer's overall debt;
  - increases today's Cash Credit Payments or MoMo Credit Payments total;
  - affects the corresponding Cash or MoMo reconciliation channel; but
  - does not contribute to Accounted Sales because the sale was counted when
    the original Credit Sale occurred.

5.4 EXPENSE
- Every Expense uses either the Cash or MoMo channel.
- Enter expense category, amount, date/time, and description.
- A Cash Expense is added back when reconstructing Cash Sales because it reduced
  the physical cash that remained in the drawer.
- A MoMo Expense reduces Expected Closing MoMo.

5.5 CANCELLATION
- Financial records are not physically deleted.
- An eligible MoMo Sale, Credit Sale, Credit Payment, or Expense can be cancelled
  with a required reason.
- The original record, cancellation reason, actor, and time remain visible.
- During an Open day, a Standard user can cancel only an eligible record they
  created; an Administrator can cancel an eligible business record.
- After Counts are submitted, normal cancellation is locked.
- In a Reopened day, correction writes are Administrator-only.


6. CUSTOMERS, INVOICES, PAYMENTS, AND ALLOCATIONS
-------------------------------------------------
6.1 CUSTOMER CLASSIFICATION
- A customer created during Credit Sale entry starts as Provisional.
- An Administrator may confirm Provisional, Occasional, or Regular status.
- Suggestions do not automatically change a classification.

6.2 CUSTOMER OUTSTANDING BALANCE
Conceptually, the customer balance is:

    Customer Outstanding Balance
      = Opening Customer Balance
      + Credit Sales
      - Credit Payments
      + Approved Debit Adjustments
      - Approved Credit Adjustments

In this version, the ledger is calculated from the retained financial records.
Cancelled records do not remain active in the current balance.

6.3 PAYMENT ALLOCATION
An allocation links part of a Credit Payment to a particular Credit Sale invoice.

Rules:
- The allocation cannot exceed the payment's unallocated remainder.
- The allocation cannot exceed the invoice's outstanding amount.
- One payment may be allocated across multiple invoices when valid.
- Allocation does not create another payment.
- Allocation does not reduce customer debt twice. The payment already reduced
  customer debt; allocation only explains which invoice it settled.

6.4 ALLOCATION REVERSAL
- An Administrator may reverse an allocation with a required reason.
- Reversal restores the invoice's outstanding amount.
- Reversal restores the payment's unallocated amount.
- It does not reverse the original customer payment.
- It does not change the customer's overall debt, the day's operational totals,
  or a previously frozen closing snapshot.


7. SUBMITTING CLOSING COUNTS
----------------------------
At the end of operations, a Standard user or Administrator enters:
- Total Cash Counted: all physical cash actually present in the drawer; and
- Actual Closing MoMo: the real wallet balance observed at closing.

After submission:
- the day moves to Counts Submitted;
- normal operational entry becomes read-only; and
- the Administrator can perform Reconciliation Review.

If the counts were wrong, the Administrator can use Return to Counts. If an
underlying operation must be corrected, the Administrator can use Return to
Operations. A reason is required and the prior submission remains in history.


8. CASH SALES CALCULATION
-------------------------
BalanceDesk calculates Cash Sales from the physical closing cash count:

    Calculated Cash Sales
      = Total Cash Counted
      - Drawer Cash
      - Drawer Coins
      - Cash Credit Payments
      + Cash Expenses

Why each part is used:
- Total Cash Counted is the cash physically present at closing.
- Drawer Cash and Drawer Coins are opening float, not today's sales.
- Cash Credit Payments are money received today for earlier Credit Sales, so
  they are cash movement but not today's sales.
- Cash Expenses reduced the cash left in the drawer, so they are added back to
  reconstruct the Cash Sales that occurred before the expenses were paid.

Example:
    Total Cash Counted           GHS 3,200.00
    Less Drawer Cash            -GHS 1,000.00
    Less Drawer Coins           -GHS    50.00
    Less Cash Credit Payments   -GHS   400.00
    Add Cash Expenses            GHS   250.00
    ------------------------------------------------
    Calculated Cash Sales        GHS 2,000.00


9. MOMO RECONCILIATION
----------------------
Expected Closing MoMo is calculated as:

    Expected Closing MoMo
      = Opening MoMo Balance
      + MoMo Sales
      + MoMo Credit Payments
      - MoMo Expenses

MoMo Variance is:

    MoMo Variance = Actual Closing MoMo - Expected Closing MoMo

Interpretation:
- Positive MoMo Variance = MoMo Overage.
- Negative MoMo Variance = MoMo Shortage.
- Zero MoMo Variance = Balanced.

Example:
    Opening MoMo                  GHS 2,500.00
    Add MoMo Sales               +GHS 1,200.00
    Add MoMo Credit Payments     +GHS   300.00
    Less MoMo Expenses           -GHS   200.00
    ------------------------------------------------
    Expected Closing MoMo         GHS 3,800.00
    Actual Closing MoMo           GHS 3,790.00
    MoMo Variance                -GHS    10.00 (Shortage)


10. ACCOUNTED SALES, OVERAGE, AND SHORTAGE
------------------------------------------
The Administrator enters Total Sales during Reconciliation Review. Total Sales
is the business's authoritative daily sales figure used for comparison.

Accounted Sales is calculated as:

    Accounted Sales
      = Calculated Cash Sales
      + MoMo Sales
      + Credit Sales

Credit Payments are not added because they settle earlier Credit Sales and would
otherwise count the same sale twice. Expenses are not direct sales; their Cash
or MoMo effects are already handled in the channel calculations.

Final Difference is:

    Difference = Accounted Sales - Total Sales

Overage and Shortage are then separated:

    Overage  = maximum(Difference, 0)
    Shortage = maximum(-Difference, 0)

Meaning:
- If Accounted Sales is greater than Total Sales, the positive difference is an
  Overage and Shortage is zero.
- If Accounted Sales is lower than Total Sales, the missing amount is a Shortage
  and Overage is zero.
- If they are equal, both Overage and Shortage are zero and the result is Balanced.

Balanced example:
    Calculated Cash Sales         GHS 2,000.00
    MoMo Sales                    GHS 1,200.00
    Credit Sales                  GHS   800.00
    ------------------------------------------------
    Accounted Sales               GHS 4,000.00
    Total Sales                   GHS 4,000.00
    Difference                    GHS     0.00
    Overage                       GHS     0.00
    Shortage                      GHS     0.00

Overage example:
    Accounted Sales               GHS 4,050.00
    Total Sales                   GHS 4,000.00
    Difference                   +GHS    50.00
    Overage                       GHS    50.00
    Shortage                      GHS     0.00

Shortage example:
    Accounted Sales               GHS 3,950.00
    Total Sales                   GHS 4,000.00
    Difference                   -GHS    50.00
    Overage                       GHS     0.00
    Shortage                      GHS    50.00

MoMo Variance and Final Difference are separate checks. MoMo Variance checks the
wallet channel. Final Difference compares total Accounted Sales with the Admin's
authoritative Total Sales figure.


11. RECONCILIATION CONFIRMATION AND FINAL CLOSE
-----------------------------------------------
The Administrator performs these as two separate actions:

RECONCILIATION REVIEW
1. Enter Total Sales.
2. Review opening values, operational totals, physical counts, Calculated Cash
   Sales, Expected and Actual Closing MoMo, MoMo Variance, Accounted Sales,
   Difference, Overage, and Shortage.
3. A non-zero difference requires the Administrator's acknowledgement. The
   explanation is optional and can be used to record helpful context.
4. Select Confirm Reconciliation.

Confirm Reconciliation does not close the day. It records the reviewed state and
allows a final check.

FINAL CLOSE
1. Review the confirmed figures again.
2. Use Final Close and confirm the operation.
3. BalanceDesk creates an immutable closing snapshot and locks the Business Day.

After Final Close:
- normal writes for that day are blocked;
- the day appears under Closed Days;
- its operational records and frozen reconciliation can be inspected later; and
- opening, counts, reconciliation, closing actor, time, and audit history remain.


12. REOPENING AND CORRECTING A CLOSED DAY
-----------------------------------------
Only an Administrator may reopen an eligible Closed Day.

Conditions:
- No other Business Day may be active.
- A reopening reason is required.
- The previous closing snapshot is retained unchanged.

Flow:
1. Open Closed Days and select the relevant day/latest snapshot.
2. Select Reopen Business Day.
3. Enter the reason and confirm.
4. Make the authorised correction.
5. Submit replacement closing counts.
6. Confirm reconciliation again.
7. Perform Final Close again.

The second close creates another snapshot version. Earlier snapshot versions stay
available and are not silently recalculated from the later corrections.


13. CLOSED DAYS AND ACTIVITY HISTORY
------------------------------------
CLOSED DAYS
- Administrator-only archive of closed Business Days.
- Shows frozen reconciliation and snapshot versions.
- Shows the paged operational ledger and correction/reopening history.

ACTIVITY HISTORY
- Administrator-only audit view.
- Includes openings, count submissions, reconciliation, closes, returns,
  reopenings, cancellations, allocation actions, customer decisions, and local
  user administration.
- Records the applicable actor and time.
- Does not display passwords, security secrets, or session data.


14. LOCAL USER AND PASSWORD FLOW
--------------------------------
- An Administrator can create Standard users.
- An Administrator can deactivate or reactivate Standard users.
- Deactivated users cannot log in.
- An Administrator can reset a Standard user's password; existing sessions for
  that Standard user are invalidated.
- Users who know their current password can change it from Profile.
- Administrator passwords cannot be reset from Local Users.


15. START, STOP, BACKUP, AND RESTORE
-----------------------------------
STARTING EACH DAY
- Double-click the desktop BalanceDesk shortcut.
- It starts the private local application and opens a maximized app window in
  Google Chrome when available, then Microsoft Edge, then your default browser.
- If it is already running, the shortcut only reopens the app window.
- Closing the app window does not delete data or stop the local server. Use the
  BalanceDesk shortcut to reopen it. Windows stops it when the computer shuts down.

STOPPING
- You do not need to stop BalanceDesk just to close its app window or shut down
  Windows.
- Run "Stop BalanceDesk.bat" inside the extracted folder before moving or
  updating that folder, backing up, restoring, or troubleshooting the server.

BACKUP
1. Run "Stop BalanceDesk.bat" inside the extracted folder.
2. Run "Backup BalanceDesk.bat".
3. BalanceDesk checks database integrity and foreign keys.
4. It creates and verifies a timestamped SQLite snapshot in the backups folder.

RESTORE
1. Run "Stop BalanceDesk.bat" inside the extracted folder.
2. Run "Restore BalanceDesk.bat".
3. Select a BalanceDesk v1.1.0 .db backup.
4. Type RESTORE exactly when requested.
5. BalanceDesk verifies the selected backup.
6. It creates a pre-restore safety backup and raw fallback.
7. It restores and verifies the active database.
8. Open BalanceDesk and confirm the expected records.

All application data, settings, backups, and logs remain local to the extracted
BalanceDesk folder. The application listens only on 127.0.0.1 and does not use a
hosted database, cloud authentication, remote API, telemetry, email, SMS,
WhatsApp, or external storage.
